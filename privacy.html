Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Who We Are

Nafs Warrior is an educational and reflective website built to help people understand their triggers, break harmful habits, and grow with intention. We take your privacy seriously.

2. Information We Collect

  • Your name or nickname — only if you choose to enter it, stored locally on your own device. We never see it.
  • Feedback responses — the tool, rating, and message you choose to send through the feedback form. This is completely anonymous.
  • Preferences (such as light/dark mode) — saved locally on your device using your browser's local storage.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To improve our tools and content based on anonymous feedback.
  • To personalise your experience (such as remembering your chosen name and theme on your device).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

4. Data Storage & Third Parties

Anonymous feedback submissions are stored securely through Firebase, a service by Google. Firebase may process this data under its own privacy policies. No personal identifiers are collected in feedback submissions.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use cookies or trackers for advertising. Your preferences are remembered through your browser's local storage, which stays on your device. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

6. Children & Young People

This website is intended for teenagers and young adults. If you are under 13, please use this site with a parent or guardian's knowledge and guidance.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. We encourage you to review it occasionally.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at: thenafswar@gmail.com
