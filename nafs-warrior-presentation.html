Slide 02 · The Mission

What is Nafs Warrior? Why does it exist?

Nafs Warrior is a free, installable web app (PWA) designed for young Muslims who sincerely want to leave haram habits but cannot find a structured, gentle, non-judgmental path that speaks their language — and actually works.

The Problem
Knowledge isn’t enough

Most young Muslims already know music, pornography, and weak prayer are problems. What they lack is a structured daily journey, a crisis plan, and someone who breaks the shame so they can act.

The Approach
Islam + real psychology

Every tool blends authentic Islamic evidence (Quran, Hadith, scholarship) with evidence-based psychology (CBT, DBT, neuroscience, habit science). Faith motivates; structure sustains.

The Promise
Gentle, never shaming

No guilt-shaming, no fear-mongering. The tone is a caring sibling: “You are not broken. You are a Muslim who is fighting.” Shame is treated as a trigger to break, not a tool.

Core belief wired into every page: “Every son of Adam sins, and the best of sinners are those who repent.” (Tirmidhi). Recovery is not perfection — it is falling less often and getting back up faster.
Slide 03 · Architecture

The Big Picture: how everything connects

One entry point, four journey types, three support layers. Every piece has a single job, and they all point back to the same goal.

Fig 1 — Nafs Warrior system architecture
Journeys (guided multi-day) Crisis rescue tools Knowledge content Support & compliance
Design principle
Self-contained pages

Every page is its own file. No server, no install — it runs from a folder or phone browser, and can be “installed” as an app via the PWA manifest.

Data principle
Progress lives on-device

Streaks and completions are saved in the user’s browser (localStorage). The app never needs an account — privacy-first, suitable for hidden/private struggles.

Slide 07 · Crisis Tools

In-the-Moment Rescue — when the urge hits

Why they exist: knowing “what” is wrong is useless at 1 AM when the urge strikes. These two tools are instant-action playbooks that interrupt the craving before it becomes an act.

The 6-Step Panic Controller
When panic or crisis strikes
  • 1. Acknowledge & label — name the emotion to the Amygdala
  • 2. Grounded — the boat metaphor (5-4-3-2-1 senses)
  • 3. Box breathing — 4-4-4-4 to calm the nervous system
  • 4. A’udhu billah — spiritual anchor & dhikr
  • 5. Reframe — CBT cognitive restructure
  • 6. Act small — one gentle next step forward
The 14-Step Urge Tool
The dedicated “urge right now” route
  • Leaves the room · senses urge surfing (ride the wave)
  • TIPP distress tolerance (temperature/intense exercise/-paced breathing · paired muscle relaxation)
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding scan
  • Surf the trigger thoughts · write the “surf log”
  • Two rak’ah + istighfar
  • Reconnect to “what kind of man do I want to be?”
  • Reminder of death & the Day of Judgment (a’mal weighed)
  • Final decision point: 30-minute delay before anything

Measure of success: whether the user followed the protocol before acting, and whether the 30-minute delay held.

Slide 09 · Measuring Success

How Do We Know It Works?

Success is not “never sin again” — it is direction. Nafs Warrior measures progress the way recovery is actually measured: honestly, incrementally, and privately.

21 days
Music Journey
Week-completion milestone

3 progress-guarded weeks. Completion = all 7 dots + full week finished; the celebration modal marks real persistence.

7 days
Purity Journey
Program completion

All 7 day-pages finished. Each day’s checklist item (e.g., trigger map filled) counts as evidence of work done, not just read.

Streak
Salah Tracker
Consecutive all-5 days

The flame — tracked in localStorage. Streaks are the strongest self-accountability signal a user can see daily.

localStorage
How it’s stored
On-device, private

Pattern: mt_week1, mt_daypos, pt_week1, pt_daypos. Each tool namespaces its keys — no account, no server, no leak.

Not tracked
Deliberate choice
Shame-free by design

No time-stamps of the last relapse, no “days since fall” widget that preaches despair. The app measures motion toward Allah, never distance from a past sin.

Lived change
What the mentor sees
Surface with the user

Because data is private, the mentor asks: How many all-5 days this week? How far did the streak go? Which day in your journey gave you the hardest fight?

Slide 10 · Summary for the Mentor

A Tool for a Real, Human Fight

One-sentence answer
What is Nafs Warrior?

A private, installable web app that walks young Muslims through structured, faith-based, psychologically-sound journeys to leave haram habits and rebuild worship — with crisis tools for the moment the urge hits.

The three layers at a glance
Journeys · Crisis · Knowledge
  • 3 journeys: Music (21d), Purity (7d), Salah (daily streak)
  • 2 crisis tools: 6-step Panic, 14-step Urge
  • 6 knowledge pages: Change, Sabr, Secret, Excuses, Substitution, Community
Design philosophy
Gentle, never shaming
  • Progress-guarded week unlocks (no skipping)
  • Qada/on-time/missed honesty, not guilt
  • Relapse is folded into the plan as a reset point, never a verdict
How to discuss this with a struggling person
Open with the question, not the answer

“What’s the one habit you wish you could change?” Then hand them the matching journey. The tools support the mentorship; the mentor still holds the role Islam gives them.

Final word: Nafs Warrior does not claim to be a replacement for scholars, therapists, or parents. It is armor and maps for a war that is fought daily, alone, at the level of the nafs — a companion for the hours nobody else sees.
