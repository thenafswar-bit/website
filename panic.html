Before you begin

This controller is a companion tool. It is not a substitute for professional mental health care.

If you are in danger of harming yourself, stop using this page and call your local emergency or crisis line now. You are not alone.

If there is someone you can call right now (a friend or a family member), call and tell them: "I'm having a panic attack. Can you stay with me until it passes?"
