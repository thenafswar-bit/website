Meet Your Excuses.

Every excuse sounds reasonable at midnight. Here is what they sound like in daylight.

“I’ll start tomorrow.”

Tomorrow is Shaytan’s favourite time zone — it never arrives. The best day to start was a year ago. The second best is today. One clean day, that’s all you need.

“It’s just one song.”

One is never just one. One song was how every relapse started. The one that matters most is the next one — and you get to choose it.

“Everyone listens.”

Everyone isn’t accountable for your heart. You were shown the truth for a reason. The ones who leave with you are the ones who matter — and Allah.

“I’m too weak to quit.”

You were never meant to do it alone. Ask the One who holds everything — including your weakness. “O Allah, it is only through Your strength that I can change.”

“I already failed this week.”

You didn’t fail the journey — you slipped. The Prophet ï·º said the best of sinners are those who repent. Mark today, get up, keep walking.
