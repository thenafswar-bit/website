About Nafs Warrior

We combine Islam, psychology and neuroscience to explore topics like habits, desires, distractions, self-control, motivation, addiction, mental wellbeing and our relationship with Allah SWT.

But Nafs Warrior isn't designed to be "too Islamic" or difficult to understand.

You don't need to already know a lot about Islam, psychology or neuroscience to use it. Everything is explained in a simple, practical and understandable way, while still taking the research seriously.

Our Research

We don't want to make claims just because they sound good.

Our content is built through both primary and secondary research.

Primary research includes information and insights we gather ourselves through surveys, experiences, discussions, observations and other forms of direct research.

Secondary research involves studying existing academic research, psychological literature, neuroscience research, Islamic sources, books and other credible material.

For Islamic content, we look at the Qur'an, authentic ahadith and scholarly explanations rather than treating random online information as Islamic evidence.

For psychology and neuroscience, we look at peer-reviewed studies, established research and credible academic sources wherever possible.

We then bring these areas together and ask:

  • What does the research actually tell us?
  • What does Islam teach us?
  • And how can we turn that knowledge into something genuinely useful?

Research → Understand → Build → Help

That is the process behind Nafs Warrior.

We research a topic, understand the evidence, turn what we learn into practical tools and resources, and share them so others can benefit.

Nafs Warrior is still growing, learning and improving. We don't claim to have every answer.

We simply want to create a space where you can learn about yourself, your nafs and your deen without feeling overwhelmed by complicated language or judged for being a work in progress.

Important Note

This is not a substitute for professional help — only a tool to assist you. Bi iznillah.
