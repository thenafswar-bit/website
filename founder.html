Founder's Note

Assalaamu Alaikum! I hope you're doing well :)

I really didn't know how to begin this note, but I want you to know that if you feel stuck in life, at the mercy of your sins and bad habits, or simply confused about life in general, you're not alone. We are more similar than you might think.

The reason I started Nafs Warrior when I was 16 was because I was struggling with sins I was too ashamed to share with others, experiencing panic attacks, not knowing how to deal with them, and so much more. I tried many things, but they didn't work out for me.

Then one day, I was in a group chat talking about how I had an idea to make something, but I had no idea how to actually implement it. There was one person in particular who motivated me and taught me all the basics. From there, I made my first website, not knowing anything about what I was doing.

And before this version of Nafs Warrior, there were many, many more. Even purple coloured ones. 😭

Allah sees you fighting those battles, dear Nafs Warrior. He sees the struggles you don't tell anyone about, the effort you make when nobody else knows, and every time you choose to keep going.

If Allah SWT allowed you to find this site among billions of people, perhaps that means something. Nothing happens by coincidence, and sometimes a small reminder can reach us at exactly the time we need it.

So keep going. Keep fighting. Keep turning back to Allah.

May Allah SWT be with you all, help you through your struggles, never let you go, and never let you forget Him. Ameen. 🤍

I'd genuinely love to hear your stories and life experiences too. Feel free to reach out to thenafswar@gmail.com to share your reflections, thoughts, ideas, experiences, or anything else — including if you'd like to volunteer or collaborate.

You're not alone in this war within.
